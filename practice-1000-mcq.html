Source & quality note

Every question is newly written from the fact-checked Complete NEC License Prep Course Notes v5.5 — none copied from the 983 MCQs of the notes banks. All questions are 1-mark level and chapters are interleaved in random order (10 from every chapter per set). All 1,000 questions were then audited line by line against the source codes and standard texts (IS 456, IS 800, IS 2720, IS 4031/1077, IS 1905, IRC, NBC and NDWQS, plus Nepal's acts and policies): the options of every question are matched in form and length so none stands out, no distractor gives the answer away, and each explanation states why the key is right and why the strongest distractor is wrong. The stored key is spread across the four positions in each set and the options are reshuffled on every render, so no letter is more likely to be correct than any other.
